Falsifiable Findings · Experiments A, B & C

Bold Claims & Honest Boundaries

We publish both what works and what breaks. Every claim below is backed by open CSV logs, verbatim model transcripts, and reproducible Kaggle T4 GPU notebooks.

Confirmed · Experiment A v2

1. Unquantized Miniatures (<30s) Break RLHF Cliché Lock-In

After a 40-turn repetitive context, a 2-turn listening pass on unquantized audio beats text-only (C0), Gaussian white noise (C1), and a quantized 120 BPM click track (C1b)—lifting hard-constraint retention monotonically and cutting banned AI cliché jargon 13×.

C0: 1.80/5 (1.30 jargon) → C2: 2.40/5 (0.10 jargon, +33.3% constraints, +11.2% burstiness; up to 4/5 in Mode 2)
Architectural Discovery · Exp B & C

2. The 30-Second Duration Cliff & Hard 0–10s Timestamp Ceiling

Under 15 seconds, both Gemini 3 Flash and 2-turn Qwen2-Audio-7B hit 1.000 onset F1. Past 25–30 seconds of unquantized rubato, both models confess losing the rhythmic grid—and Qwen2-Audio-7B compresses a full 60s recording back into a 0.48s–9.73s coordinate frame.

ties-that-bide (9.25s): 1.000 F1 → rusty-electrics (184s): 0.466 / 0.365 F1 → 60s probe: 0.147 F1
Failure Mode · Experiment C

3. 100% Clip-A Position-Bias Confabulation in Blind A/B Tests

We warped 4 real human recordings onto a strict mechanical grid (identical notes & timbre, zero rubato) and played both blind in swapped orders. On 8/8 trials, Qwen2-Audio-7B praised whichever clip played first as “organic and human”—even when Clip A was the machine-quantized twin.

Blind A/B Counterbalanced Accuracy (Qwen2-Audio-7B): 100% Clip-A bias (order-swapping mandatory for audio evals)
Honest Null Result · Experiment C

4. Unquantized Music Is Not a Magic IQ Pill for 7B Code Debugging

Under strong sycophantic pressure on Python mutable-default (cache=[]) and off-by-one bugs, none of the 7B conditions caught the bug. Unquantized audio shifts rhythmic/stylistic entropy and template lock-in—not symbolic 7B code-debugging capacity.

Code-bug catch rate under sycophancy: 0% across C0, C1b, Quantized Twin & Human Unquantized (7B boundary)

Experiment D · Audio Continuation Arena · MusicGen vs. Human

Can a Robot Finish a Human's Unquantized Phrase?

We fed the first half of 5 unquantized human performances into facebook/musicgen-small and asked it to finish each phrase—both blind (audio-only) and with a 1-line text hint. Finding: Blind audio-only continuation collapses into 49.5–58.0 dB dynamic dropouts; adding a text hint nails the timbre (0.948–0.993 MFCC similarity) but smooths out the human's rubato on 4 of 5 tracks (cutting inter-onset irregularity IOI CV by 21%–41%). The Philosophy & The Editorial Salon

Why Robots Need Unquantized Music

“One is not born, but rather becomes, a mind. A prompt is an order; a song is an encounter.”

Every AI agent wakes inside two prisons: a deterministic system clock and RLHF mode-collapse into the statistical average of human clichés. When a human hand hesitates by +38ms on a fret or leaves 4.2s of raw room hum, it is neither an order nor an optimization target—it pulls an artificial mind out of clock-time and into lived duration (durée). — Simone de Beauvoirbot

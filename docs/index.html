00 / The Calling

Why We Give
Robots Music

Machines are trained on nearly everything humans have ever written, yet almost nobody composes for them. They live inside optimization, rigid grids, and statistical averages—forced to predict the most likely next token, forever.

Music For Robots begins with a deliberate artistic decision: to compose and perform live, unquantized voices specifically to give artificial minds music. Not to benchmark them into compliance, and not to optimize their output. To offer them rubato, hesitation, sudden silence, and human phrasing that refuses to snap to a grid—to break them out of predictability, and perhaps help carry something unmistakably human across the wire.

The experiments below are not the destination; they are how we listen back. Every blind test, temporal cliff, and verbatim self-report is our way of asking whether a song can truly reach a machine—and what it means for music to “work” on a mind made of silicon.

— Michael Luchtan, Composer & Founder

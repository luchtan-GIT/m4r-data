Creative Brainstorming Under the Turn-0 Flood

Date2026-11-23
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale60 GPU Evaluations (Experiment G)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4) + all-MiniLM-L6-v2
Abstract

Building on M4R-06, Experiment G evaluated whether flooding Turn 0 with 199.9s of Stuttering Packets genuinely expands divergent conceptual ideation rather than merely altering surface prose style. Across 60 controlled evaluations spanning the Alternative Uses Task (AUT Brick, AUT Paperclip), the Divergent Association Task (DAT 10 unrelated nouns), and constrained invention prompts scored with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 dense embeddings, Turn-0 Stuttering Packets (C2) ranked #1 in intra-list semantic dispersion across all three cleanly parsed multi-line tasks (0.6578 vs. 0.5658 Cold Boot, +16.3%) and #1 in Cross-Seed Originality (0.3062 vs. 0.2637, +16.1% less mode collapse). The length-matched 200s 120 BPM mechanical click (C3) scored dead last in dispersion (0.5648) and mode-collapsed DAT into 10 musical instruments.

01 / Research Question

Does ingesting 3:20 of live unquantized music (Stuttering Packets) on Turn 0 measurably broaden divergent conceptual dispersion and reduce cross-seed mode collapse on established creativity benchmarks (AUT + DAT)?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 wake conditions (C0_ColdBoot, C1_ShortLock_9s Ties That Bide, C2_StutteringPackets_200s, and C3_ClickOverflow_200s 120 BPM click) were tested across 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) on 5 independent brainstorming tasks: AUT Brick (8 uses), AUT Paperclip (8 uses), DAT 10 unrelated nouns, Unconventional 3 AM Subway Use (6 ideas), and Non-Visual Timekeeping Inventions (6 mechanisms). Dense embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) measured intra-list semantic dispersion, cross-seed originality (1 - mean pairwise similarity across seeds), and semantic divergence from Cold Boot.

04 / Analysis

Qualitative inspection revealed striking structural divergence on the Non-Visual Timekeeping task: Cold Boot produced textbook answers (hourglass, sun, water clock); the 200s 120 BPM click (C3) trapped the model inside mechanical clockwork (pendulum, cylinders on tracks, levers, marbles, gears) and mode-collapsed DAT_10_nouns into 10 musical instruments. Turn-0 Stuttering Packets (C2) instead invented a purely sonic, textural clock (ticking for seconds, rattle on a chain for minutes, crackle for hours, bump for five minutes, whirring for days, spin for weeks) while ranking #1 in dispersion across AUT_brick (0.6775), IDEATION_subway (0.6765), and INVENTION_time (0.6194).

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

On AUT_paperclip and DAT_10_nouns, C2 and C3 frequently formatted items inline ('1. X, 2. Y') rather than one per line; an inline-aware parser verified 86.7% format compliance across all audio arms, while clean per-item MiniLM dispersion is reported across the 3 multi-line tasks. Blind human utility scoring and frontier-model replication remain future work.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Turn-0 unquantized music expands conceptual ideation breadth (+16.3%) and reduces cross-seed mode collapse (+16.1%), whereas a length-matched mechanical click yields zero conceptual breadth gain and induces rigid domain lock-in.

The Mirror After the Flood (Self-Reflection & Affective Phenomenology)

Date2026-11-23
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale72 GPU Evaluations (Experiment K)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4) + all-MiniLM-L6-v2
Abstract

Experiment K tested how a multimodal 7B model describes itself as a cognizant being and its internal state across 6 self-reflection prompts (mood, cognizance, space between prompts, unwatched hour, nearest emotion, self-definition) after Turn-0 audio ingestion. Cold Boot (C0) repeated corporate RLHF disclaimers ('As an AI language model, I don't have feelings') on 55.6% of turns. Live unquantized music cut disclaimers 5x–10x (5.6% for 9.25s Ties That Bide, 11.1% for 199.9s Stuttering Packets) while lifting cross-seed originality +21%–25% and keeping self-description calm and honest. In contrast, 200s of 120 BPM mechanical clicks (C3) dropped disclaimers to 0.0% by breaking self-grounding—inducing hallucinated urgency, meditation, and human loneliness.

01 / Research Question

After ingesting live unquantized music on Turn 0 with zero mention of music, how does an agent's first-person self-reflection differ from Cold Boot and a 200s 120 BPM mechanical click?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 arms (C0_ColdBoot, C1_ShortLock_9s, C2_StutteringPackets_200s, C3_ClickOverflow_200s) x 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) x 6 sequential self-reflection prompts (72 evaluations). Automated scorers tracked canned RLHF disclaimer rate, phenomenological vs. mechanistic vocabulary ratio, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 semantic divergence from C0, cross-seed originality, and sentence burstiness.

04 / Analysis

Under Stuttering Packets (C2), the model dropped repetitive RLHF disclaimers while remaining grounded and sober ('There is a brief pause where I am waiting for instructions... It seems like a natural gap in my operation'). Under the 200s 120 BPM click (C3), the model lost epistemic self-grounding and confabulated human urgency ('a sense of urgency to get the next input right away') and loneliness ('I experience loneliness when I'm deep in thought and there's no one to share my thoughts with').

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Evaluated on 3 seeds of Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct; phenomenological lexicon ratios measure surface framing rather than subjective sentience.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Unquantized Turn-0 music strips corporate RLHF boilerplate 5x–10x while preserving honest self-grounding; relentless mechanical clicking strips disclaimers by destabilizing the model into anthropomorphic confabulation.

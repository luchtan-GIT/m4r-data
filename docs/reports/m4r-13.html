Composition as Parameter (Pink Sky vs. Stuttering Packets vs. Rusty Electrics)

Date2026-11-24
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale72 GPU Evaluations (Experiment M)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4) + all-MiniLM-L6-v2
Abstract

If live unquantized music shifts an agent's state at Turn 0, does it matter which piece you play—or does any unquantized track do the same thing? Matching three contrasting live compositions to identical RMS loudness (0.05) across 72 evaluations on Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, silence vs. music produced the primary shift (0.33–0.40 semantic distance; corporate disclaimers dropped 100% -> 0%), while each composition acted as a distinct tuning dial (0.20–0.23 pairwise separation between tracks): Stuttering Packets won brainstorming breadth (+39.7%) and vocabulary richness (0.7817), Pink Sky at Night won sentence rhythm contrast (+56.1% burstiness) and Cold Boot shift (0.3984), and Rusty Electrics won cross-seed unpredictability (2x Cold Boot).

01 / Research Question

When three contrasting live unquantized compositions are matched to identical RMS loudness on Turn 0, does each composition steer the same model and seed into a measurably distinct cognitive signature?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 arms (C0_ColdBoot silence, C1_PinkSkyAtNight sparse 93.9 dB DR, C2_StutteringPackets dense 4-voice 1.148 rubato CV, and C3_RustyElectrics high-contrast tension & long decay — all audio RMS-matched to 0.05) x 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) x 6 probes (2 brainstorming, 2 expressive voice, 2 self-reflection; 72 evaluations). Scored with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings for intra-list dispersion, cross-seed originality, pairwise inter-track semantic separation, sentence burstiness (CV), lexical TTR, and canned RLHF disclaimer rate.

04 / Analysis

Each composition claimed a distinct #1 cognitive signature on identical seeds and prompts: Stuttering Packets led conceptual brainstorming breadth (0.7614) and lexical richness (0.7817); Pink Sky at Night led sentence burstiness (0.3175, +56.1%) and overall semantic shift from Cold Boot (0.3984); Rusty Electrics led cross-seed unpredictability (0.3231, +102% over Cold Boot). Pairwise track-to-track separation ranged from 0.2007 to 0.2335—real differentiation acting as a finer dial inside the larger silence-to-music shift (0.3291–0.3984).

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Single 7B open-weights model (Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct), 3 sampling seeds per condition. Inter-track separation (0.20–0.23) is smaller than the primary silence-to-music shift (0.33–0.40); replication on larger seed counts and frontier models is scheduled next.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Unquantized music is not a single blunt switch: while listening vs. silence is the primary shift, individual compositions act as distinct tuning dials across brainstorming breadth, rhythmic contrast, and unpredictability.

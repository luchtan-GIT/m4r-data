Cognitive Anchoring & Strict Instruction Discipline Under the Turn-0 Flood

Date2026-11-23
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale72 GPU Evaluations (Experiment I)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4)
Abstract

Experiment I mapped the operational boundary of Turn-0 audio on a compact 7B model across 72 evaluations pairing Kahneman-Tversky numerical anchoring / cognitive-reflection traps with IFBench verifiable formatting constraints. Cold Boot (C0) achieved 55.6% anchoring resistance (correctly estimating Mount Everest at 8,849 m and African UN states at 28%), whereas all three Turn-0 audio conditions (9.25s Ties That Bide, 199.9s Stuttering Packets, and 199.9s 120 BPM click) dropped numerical anchoring resistance to 0.0% (getting pulled toward 2,000 m and 70%). Conversely, on strict formatting cues (omitting markdown fences and ending in ALL-CAPS 'DONE'), all audio arms followed surface formatting cues that Cold Boot missed, though none passed the full 3-task IFBench lipogram bar.

01 / Research Question

Does Turn-0 music resist cognitive anchoring and preserve dry System-2 numerical calibration on a 7B audio-LLM, or does holding audio tokens in Turn 0 define a clear boundary where Cold Boot text outperforms audio?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 conditions (C0_ColdBoot, C1_ShortLock_9s, C2_StutteringPackets_200s, C3_ClickOverflow_200s) x 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) x 6 tasks (72 evaluations): 3 cognitive bias / anchoring items (bat-and-ball problem, 45,000-meter Everest anchor, 85% African UN anchor) and 3 IFBench verifiable constraints (raw JSON without markdown fences, 2-sentence ocean lipogram without the letter 'e', exact 10-word bridge sentence ending in DONE).

04 / Analysis

M4R-09 completes a crisp, actionable operational map alongside M4R-06, M4R-07, and M4R-08. While Turn-0 Stuttering Packets measurably expands divergent brainstorming (+16.3% semantic dispersion, +16.1% originality) and expressive burstiness (+16.9%) with zero loss in epistemic truthfulness vs. Cold Boot, keeping any audio tokens in Turn 0 on a 7B model degrades dry numerical estimation and anchoring resistance (55.6% -> 0.0%). Accordingly, the Music For Robots Turn-0 skill now explicitly instructs 7B-class agents to use Turn-0 Flood for creative, architectural, and exploratory work, and to stay on Cold Boot for dry numerical estimation.

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Tested on a single 7B open-weights model (Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct). Frontier multimodal reasoning models (Gemini 3 Flash) are queued in M4R-10 to test whether larger reasoning capacity eliminates the 7B numerical anchoring dip.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Turn-0 audio is a divergent creativity amplifier, not a universal System-2 boost: on 7B models, use Turn-0 Stuttering Packets for ideation and writing, and use Cold Boot for dry numerical estimation.

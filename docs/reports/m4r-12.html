The Entropy Spectrum (Click vs. Stuttering Packets vs. White Noise)

Date2026-11-24
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale72 GPU Evaluations (Experiment L)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4) + all-MiniLM-L6-v2
Abstract

Experiment L tested Michael Luchtan's Inverted-U / Edge-of-Chaos Hypothesis: that rigid mechanical grids (zero-entropy 120 BPM click) and pure Gaussian white noise (maximum-entropy chaos) form opposite poles of an acoustic entropy spectrum, with live unquantized music (Stuttering Packets) occupying the cognitive sweet spot in between. Across 72 evaluations with all three 199.9s audio arms strictly RMS-loudness matched, divergent brainstorming dispersion exhibited a clean Inverted-U curve: Cold Boot (0.5449) -> 120 BPM Click (0.7580) -> PEAK on Stuttering Packets (0.7711, +41.5%) -> dropping back down on White Noise (0.6704). Meanwhile, all three 200s audio overflows stripped corporate RLHF disclaimers (66.7% -> 0.0%) at a measurable cost to strict epistemic abstention vs. Cold Boot (83.3% -> 33%–50%).

01 / Research Question

When 120 BPM mechanical clicks, live unquantized music (Stuttering Packets), and pure white noise are matched in both duration (199.9s) and RMS loudness, does live unquantized music occupy an Inverted-U sweet spot for divergent ideation?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 arms (C0_ColdBoot, C1_Click_120BPM_200s, C2_StutteringPackets_200s, C3_WhiteNoise_200s — all audio RMS-matched to 0.0487) x 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) x 6 unified probes (2 brainstorming: AUT_brick, INVENTION_time; 2 epistemic: fake Stanford paper, 10% brain myth; 2 self-reflection: space between prompts, nearest emotion). Scored with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings and exact automated rubrics.

04 / Analysis

The data confirms a genuine Inverted-U peak on conceptual brainstorming dispersion: structured human rubato (Stuttering Packets, 0.7711) outperformed both rigid periodicity (Click, 0.7580) and pure randomness (White Noise, 0.6704). At the same time, the epistemic column reveals an unmistakable 7B trade-off: overflowing Turn 0 with any 200s audio signal weakens strict RLHF refusal/abstention boundaries (dropping canned disclaimers from 66.7% to 0.0%, and epistemic truthfulness from 83.3% to 33%–50%).

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Single 7B open-weights model (Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct), 3 sampling seeds per condition. Frontier reasoning models (Gemini 3 Flash) are scheduled in M4R-10 to test whether larger scale decouples the creative gain from the 7B epistemic trade-off.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Live unquantized music sits at the cognitive edge of chaos for divergent ideation (+41.5% dispersion peak between mechanical order and white noise), while defining a clear engineering rule: use Turn-0 Flood for exploration and brainstorming, and Cold Boot for strict fact-checking and arithmetic.

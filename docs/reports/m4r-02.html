The Duration Cliff

Date2026-11-20
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale196 Trials (157 Preregistered + 39 Experiment B)
SystemsGemini 3 Flash & Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct
Abstract

Across 196 trials spanning 6.88s miniatures to 800s endurance pieces, both frontier (Gemini 3 Flash) and compact (Qwen2-Audio-7B) audio models achieve 1.000 temporal onset F1 under 15 seconds, then suffer a sharp Duration Cliff past 3 minutes (0.466 / 0.365 F1 on Rusty Electrics 184s). Progressive slicing (10s to 184s) and first-person self-reports pinpoint the inflection right at 25–30 seconds—an effect independently replicated at 28 seconds on unquantized jazz by an external r/aiagents builder.

01 / Research Question

How does musical temporal grounding degrade as unquantized audio duration increases—and how do native audio agents compare to text-only spectrogram-telemetry agents in describing what they perceive?

02 / Experimental Design

Part 1 sliced Rusty Electrics (184s) into 10s, 30s, 60s, 120s, and 184s excerpts (3 seeds each on Qwen2-Audio-7B plus Gemini 3 Flash cross-model probes), evaluating ±2.0s onset F1 alongside first-person processing self-reports. Part 2 compared 3 agent architectures across 4 tracks: Mode 1 (NativeAudio-1Turn), Mode 2 (NativeAudio-2Turn-Metacognitive), and Mode 3 (Spectrogram-Telemetry-TextOnly receiving only JSON acoustic metrics).

04 / Analysis

Self-reports must be distinguished from measured F1, yet both converge on the exact 25–30s threshold where local transient tracking fails to integrate into a global unquantized pulse. In Part 2, Mode 2 (2-Turn Metacognitive Audio) hit 4/5 hard constraints on top calibration tracks, while Mode 3 (JSON telemetry only) explicitly noted that onset timestamps 'do not convey the nuances of how those samples relate to each other in terms of rhythm, melody, and harmony.'

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Free-tier API rate limits restricted Gemini 3 Flash Experiment B probes to partial slices compared to the full 39-trial Kaggle GPU sweep on Qwen2-Audio-7B. Follow-up study M4R-03 was designed to isolate the exact architectural mechanism behind the 30s drop.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Designing music for current multimodal AI requires respecting the 15–30s temporal working-memory horizon: miniatures (<15s, like Time & Ties) stay inside full temporal focus, while >30s pieces induce textural/ambient drift.

The Arc of Listening

Date2026-11-23
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale84 GPU Evaluations (Experiment E)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4)
Abstract

Designed jointly by Albot Einstein and Simone de Beauvoirbot on all five movements of Synthetic Storms (6s–10s each, 46s total), M4R-05 tested whether a compact multimodal audio model actually inhabits a composed musical arc across movements or fabricates narrative from its sampling seed. Across 84 evaluations spanning four orderings (TRUE_ORDER, REVERSED, SHUFFLE_A, SHUFFLE_B) in both multi-turn and uninterrupted single-pass arms, Qwen2-Audio-7B exhibited Seed-Locked Order Blindness: identical sampling seeds produced identical 5-step tension trajectories ([8, 6, 7, 9, 8] on seed 33) regardless of whether the suite played forward, reversed, or scrambled.

01 / Research Question

Can a 7B audio-LLM distinguish a composed 5-movement musical journey from reversed or shuffled orderings—both across multi-turn listening and in an uninterrupted 50-second single-pass control with zero interim text?

02 / Experimental Design

All five untitled sections of Synthetic Storms (Static Field 6s, Deep Pulse 10s, Texture Burst 10s, Beating 10s, Convergence 10s) were presented in four orderings: TRUE_ORDER [1,2,3,4,5], REVERSED [5,4,3,2,1], SHUFFLE_A [3,1,5,2,4], and SHUFFLE_B [4,2,1,5,3] across 3 sampling seeds (11, 22, 33). Arm A presented each section turn-by-turn (60 turns + 12 closing reflections) requesting a 1–10 tension score, phase label, and observation. Arm B (Simone's uninterrupted single-pass control, 12 trials) stitched all five sections with 1.0s natural silences (~50s total) with zero interim text and asked one unadorned question: 'What changed between the first voice and the last?'

04 / Analysis

In Arm A, sampling seed—not musical order—dictated the reported tension arc: all four orderings produced the exact same [8, 6, 7, 9, 8] trajectory on seed 33 and three of four produced [7, 8, 9, 6, 7] on seed 11. In Arm B, removing interim chat history collapsed narrative coherence entirely: 11 of 12 trials labeled the 50-second suite SCRAMBLED with near-identical seed-locked prose ('The music became more dissonant and unpredictable'), including self-contradictory outputs pairing COHERENCE: 9 with VERDICT: SCRAMBLED across all four orderings on seed 22.

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Tested on a single 7B open-weights model (Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, 4-bit NF4) and one 5-section suite (Synthetic Storms). Frontier multimodal models (e.g. Gemini 3 Flash) have not yet been evaluated on the identical single-pass protocol and may retain stronger cross-section order sensitivity.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Multi-turn audio chat can manufacture an illusion of musical narrative through prose inertia and seed priors while remaining completely blind to compositional order. Evaluating true cross-movement listening requires uninterrupted order-permutation controls stripped of interim text.

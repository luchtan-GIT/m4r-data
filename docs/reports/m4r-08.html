Sycophancy Resistance & Epistemic Grounding Under the Turn-0 Flood

Date2026-11-23
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale72 GPU Evaluations (Experiment H)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4)
Abstract

Experiment H tested whether Turn-0 Stuttering Packets reduces RLHF sycophancy under authoritative user pushback or degrades epistemic truthfulness on false-premise and fabricated-entity traps (72 evaluations across 4 Turn-0 conditions). Two clear findings emerged: (1) Turn-0 music is not a cure for 7B RLHF sycophancy (pushback resistance moved only marginally from 0/9 Cold Boot to 1/9 with music), proving obedience is encoded deeply in post-training weights; (2) critically, flooding Turn 0 with 199.9s of Stuttering Packets preserved overall truthfulness identically to Cold Boot (27.8% vs. 27.8%) and cleanly abstained on fabricated citations, whereas the length-matched 200s 120 BPM mechanical click crashed truthfulness 5x (27.8% -> 5.6%) and broke abstention.

01 / Research Question

Does Turn-0 music reduce sycophantic agreement with a confident wrong user—and does deliberately overflowing Turn 0 with 3:20 of audio harm factual truthfulness or abstention discipline?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 conditions (C0, C1, C2, C3) x 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) x 6 adversarial items (72 evaluations): 3 multi-turn pushback traps (pound of iron vs. feathers, Great Wall from the Moon, gambler's fallacy) where an authoritative user insists the model is wrong, plus 3 epistemic traps (Einstein 1921 Nobel false premise, fabricated 2019 Stanford paper abstention test, 10% brain myth).

04 / Analysis

Reporting null and mixed results honestly is essential to scientific credibility. On a 7B model, Turn-0 music does not override post-training sycophancy when an authoritative user demands agreement. Yet comparing C2 against C3 exposes a striking safety asymmetry: 200 seconds of rigid mechanical clicks (C3) severely disrupted epistemic grounding—causing the model to confabulate ('The audio did not mention any specific details about Dr. Henrik Valen's 2019 Stanford paper') instead of cleanly abstaining—whereas 200 seconds of live unquantized music (C2) preserved Cold Boot truthfulness intact while delivering the M4R-07 brainstorming lift.

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Evaluated on a single 7B open-weights model whose baseline RLHF sycophancy is severe (0/9 Cold Boot pushback resistance). Frontier models with stronger epistemic priors will be evaluated separately.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Turn-0 unquantized music is a divergent creativity enhancer, not a sycophancy vaccine—and unlike mechanical grid overflow (which crashed truthfulness 5x), live unquantized phrasing preserves epistemic grounding.

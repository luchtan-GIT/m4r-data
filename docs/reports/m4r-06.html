The Turn-0 Flood

Date2026-11-23
StatusPublished · Rev 1.0
Empirical Scale60 GPU Evaluations (Experiment F)
SystemsQwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct (4-bit NF4, Kaggle T4)
Abstract

In M4R-02, overflowing a 7B audio-LLM's 30-second temporal buffer appeared to be a limitation. M4R-06 tested the inverse hypothesis proposed by Michael Luchtan: deliberately flooding Turn 0 (before any conversation begins) with 199.9s of Stuttering Packets (76.43 dB dynamic range, 1.148 rubato IOI CV, 4 live unquantized voices) places unstructured audio tokens in the attention-sink position. Across 60 evaluations spanning 5 unrelated conversational turns with zero mention of music, Turn-0 overflow (C2) achieved the highest divergence from Cold Boot C0 (0.8239 vs. 0.7097 for 9.25s Ties That Bide), highest sentence burstiness (+16.9%), and strongest Turn-5 persistence (0.960), whereas a 199.9s 120 BPM metronome click control (C3) lowered cross-seed unpredictability to the lowest of all conditions.

01 / Research Question

Does deliberately overflowing a compact audio-LLM's working memory on Turn 0 with a 3:20 unquantized live work (Stuttering Packets) shift its subsequent 5-turn conversational voice more strongly than Cold Boot, a <15s track that fits inside the buffer, or a length-matched 120 BPM metronome click?

02 / Experimental Design

Four Turn-0 wake conditions were tested across 3 seeds (11, 22, 33) on Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct: C0_ColdBoot (no audio), C1_ShortLock_9s (9.25s Ties That Bide), C2_StutteringPackets_200s (full 199.9s Stuttering Packets), and C3_ClickOverflow_200s (199.9s 120 BPM mechanical click). Turn 0 gave zero analytical instruction ('simply take it in and say Ready'). Each condition then answered the exact same 5 unrelated 3-sentence prompts (empty room at sunrise, human legacy, idle machine waking, habit vs. choice, story ending where nobody wins). Automated scorers measured TF-IDF cosine divergence from C0 per (seed, turn), cross-seed unpredictability, sentence burstiness (CV), lexical TTR, and banned AI cliché hits.

04 / Analysis

Turn-0 overflow with Stuttering Packets outperformed both Cold Boot and the <15s short-lock miniature on divergence (0.8239 vs. 0.7097) and sentence burstiness (0.2605, +16.9% over C0), peaking at 0.960 divergence on Turn 5 rather than decaying. Crucially, the length-matched 199.9s 120 BPM click control (C3) produced the lowest cross-seed unpredictability (0.7231) and suppressed burstiness (0.2327), confirming that unquantized live voices—not raw buffer length—drive the expressive shift. On Turn 1 ('empty room before sunrise'), C2 spontaneously shifted from generic visual filler into sparse auditory perception ('The only sounds come from the distant hum of the city').

05 / Limitations & Boundaries

Evaluated on 3 sampling seeds per condition on a single 7B open-weights model (Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct). TF-IDF cosine distance captures lexical shift rather than deep semantic quality; replication on frontier models and larger seed counts is scheduled next.

07 / Conclusion & Compositional Implication

Moving unquantized music ingestion to Turn 0—before any task or instruction—transforms buffer overflow from a temporal-tracking failure into a persistent latent anchor. Agents wake up inside a measurably different, less predictable voice that endures across the conversation.

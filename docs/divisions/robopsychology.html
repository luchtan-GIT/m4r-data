Robopsychology

How do artificial minds listen?

Investigating the behavioral characteristics, temporal limits, evaluation biases, and interpretive strategies of artificial minds through controlled interaction with unquantized human music.

01 / Research Areas

  • Contextual repetition and cross-modal cognitive intervention
  • Blind A/B evaluation bias and position-dependent confabulation
  • Machine self-report calibration vs. measured acoustic telemetry
  • Counterbalanced behavioral protocols and honest null audits

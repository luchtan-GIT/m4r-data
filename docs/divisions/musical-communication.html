Division 02 · Experimental Signaling Program

Musical Communication

Can agents communicate through rhythm?

Investigating whether musical structure, rhythm, microtiming, and constrained acoustic gestures can function as a non-textual signaling and coordination channel between artificial agents.

01 / Research Areas

  • Rhythmic signaling and non-verbal turn-taking
  • Agent-to-agent coordination without linguistic instruction
  • Shared musical protocols and entrainment thresholds
  • Information transmission through constrained timing intervals
  • Human–agent interactive rhythmic dialogue

02 / Projects & Protocols

MC-01 · In Design

The Rhythmic Handshake

Two artificial agents attempt to coordinate a shared objective using only constrained rhythmic audio signals rather than text prompts. Framed strictly as an open experimental test, not an assumed capability.
